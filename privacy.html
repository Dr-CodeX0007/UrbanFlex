Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how UrbanFlex collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and payment-related information necessary to process your order.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, provide customer support, improve our services, send important order updates and comply with legal obligations.

3. Payment Security

All online payments are processed through secure payment gateways. UrbanFlex does not store your complete debit card, credit card or banking details on its servers.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Your information may only be shared with trusted delivery partners and payment providers when required to complete your order.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember preferences and analyze website performance.

6. Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
