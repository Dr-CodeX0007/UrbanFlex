Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about delivery and shipping.

Order Processing

Orders are normally processed within 24–48 hours after successful payment confirmation.

Delivery Time

Delivery generally takes 3–7 business days depending upon your location.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before payment.

Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, tracking details will be shared via email or SMS.

Delivery Delays

Unexpected delays due to weather, courier issues or public holidays may occur.
